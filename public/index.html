About

بلغوا عني ولو آية وحدثوا عن بني إسرائيل ولا حرج، ومن كذب علي متعمدًا فليتبوأ مقعده من النار

"Convey from me even an Ayah of the Qur'an; relate traditions from Banu Israel, and there is no restriction on that; but he who deliberately forges a lie against me let him have his abode in the Hell."

— Al-Bukhari (Riyad as-Salihin 1380)

Even an Ayah

An AI-native application designed to help you memorize the Quran one ayah at a time through a structured learning process.

Features:

  • Step-by-step memorization process
  • Audio recitation from renowned reciters
  • Handwriting practice for better retention
  • Harakat (vowel marks) learning
  • Root word etymology and tafsir
  • Progress tracking
